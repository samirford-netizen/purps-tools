All three lines move with the Stop, Activate and Trail presets, and all are today's stock prices. Each session the stop and activation lines drift away from your direction (decay), so the stock has to travel farther to reach activation. The floor is the minimum: if the option keeps climbing past activation, the trail rises with it.

Model: Black-Scholes, 4% rate, no dividends, implied vol backed out from the bid/ask mid unless you enter one. Option values are estimates, not quotes. IV moves and wide spreads will shift real fills. Your inputs are saved in this browser only.

    
Contract fit: stand-still = theta ÷ delta ÷ daily ATR, the daily move the stock needs just to keep the option flat. Trend factor = weekly ATR ÷ daily ATR. Thresholds come from the Oct 2026 stop lab (622 past trades): delta under 0.35 and stand-still 0.5+ lost money; carries paid on names with a trend factor of 3+. They're candidates, still being tested on new trades.
